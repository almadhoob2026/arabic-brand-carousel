# Design decision record / سجل القرارات التصميمية

A persistent decision record for a specific brand or campaign. Replace placeholders per project.

- Campaign goal and desired action: [replace]
- Visual direction: `editorial` / `educational` / `luxury` / `bold` / `heritage`
- Reason for direction: [tie to audience, subject, and brand]
- DESIGN_VARIANCE (1–10): 7
- VISUAL_DENSITY (1–10): 4
- CONTRAST_LEVEL (1–10): 8
- TYPOGRAPHIC_EXPRESSION (1–10): 8
- DECORATION_LEVEL (1–10): 3
- Approved layout variations: [cover, statement, steps, comparison etc.]
- Decision exceptions: [specify]
- Review date and approval: [optional]

The sliders guide choices, they are **not** scientific aesthetic measures. Never silently replace brand colors with generic preset colors.
