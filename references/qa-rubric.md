# 100-point review rubric

| Domain | Points | What earns full marks |
|---|---:|---|
| Content & narrative | 25 | clear promise; one idea per slide; progression; concrete reader value; appropriate CTA |
| Visual design & brand | 25 | accurate brand tokens; purposeful variation; balance; whitespace; consistent chrome |
| Arabic typography & readability | 20 | proper RTL and glyph joining; accurate register; readable at phone size; no clipping |
| Language & evidence | 15 | clean Arabic; verified sourced claims; examples distinguished; no misleading causality |
| Export & reproducibility | 15 | native resolution; accurate ordering; accessible edit source; PNG set; no broken assets |

## Mandatory failures
Any cut-off headline, illegible critical text, wrong logo, invented factual source, incorrect dimensions, missing slide or materially distorted Arabic = **not accepted** regardless of numeric score.

## Process
1. Review narrative and claim ledger.
2. Audit image dimensions/slide sequence and overflow signals.
3. Visually inspect **every PNG** and contact sheet, including mobile size.
4. Assign deductions with reasons and specific corrective actions.
5. Revise and repeat until >=90 and no mandatory failures.

This rubric is a human quality-control aid, not a machine-certified rating. The script's JSON report covers technical checks only.
