# Visual directions / التوجهات البصرية

Each direction is a **behavioral guideline**, not a template or a fixed palette.

| Direction | Layout character | Typography | Appropriate for | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| editorial | thoughtful asymmetric rhythm | strong title hierarchy | thought leadership, narratives | identical cards every slide |
| educational | consistent teaching sequence | clear secondary text | guides, procedures, evidence | dense copy and unreadable charts |
| luxury | ample negative space | restraint, intentional emphasis | premium brands | unnecessary glow and ornaments |
| bold | strong contrasts and large messaging | punchy headlines | high-impact campaigns | sacrificing legibility for drama |
| heritage | modern geometry with meaningful cultural references | culturally appropriate forms | cultural/heritage projects | generic ornament with no connection to content |

Directions do not create images by themselves. A licensed logo or photograph must still be supplied or approved. Do not appropriate copyrighted layouts or brand identities.
