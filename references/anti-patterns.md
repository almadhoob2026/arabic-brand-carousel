# Anti-generic design audit — avoid dogma

Separate measurable defects from subjective design choices.

## Objective blockers

- Clipped essential text, broken Arabic joining or mirrored mixed LTR/RTL tokens
- Insufficient primary text contrast; a logo replaced by an invented mark
- Incorrect dimensions, missing slide, unsafe evidence or fabricated citation

## Soft warnings (context dependent)

- Three consecutive uses of the same decorative treatment
- Cards nested in cards with no information hierarchy
- Identical layout family across every content slide
- Decorative glow or gradient unrelated to the story
- Auto-generated enthusiasm, meaningless symbols or generic CTA

Avoid categorically prohibiting Arial, Inter, gradients, cards, black, animations, or any design token: they may be valid if they are part of a brand. Choose by fit, not taste dogma.

## Critique protocol

1. Record location and observable evidence.
2. Classify severity: blocker / warning / advice.
3. State one concrete revision and potential tradeoff.
4. Re-render and visually inspect before claiming resolution.
5. A numeric rating is an **estimate**, not a machine-certified quality score.
